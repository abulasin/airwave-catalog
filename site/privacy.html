Airwave Atlas Privacy Policy

Maplewire Labs · Last updated November 13, 2026

Airwave Atlas is a radio app made by Maplewire Labs. We don't ask for your name, email or location, we don't show ads, and we don't use analytics or tracking. This page explains the little data the app does handle.

What stays on your iPhone

Your favorites, recent stations, stations you add yourself, recordings, alarm settings and app settings are stored only on your iPhone. We never receive them. Deleting the app deletes them.

Services the app talks to

To work, the app connects to other services. Like any internet connection, these services can see your device's IP address. We don't send them anything that identifies you.

  • Radio stations. When you play or record a station, your iPhone connects directly to that station's stream.
  • Station directory. The station list is downloaded from our catalog on GitHub Pages and from the public Radio Browser directory (radio-browser.info). When you play a station, the app tells Radio Browser that the station was played, so it can count popular stations. This includes only the station, not you.
  • Station details. When you open a station's details, the app may look up the station on Wikipedia and Wikidata, and read the station's own website.
  • Translation. Station names are translated by Apple on your iPhone.

Purchases

Airwave Atlas Plus is sold through the App Store. Apple handles payment; we never see your payment details. To check whether you have Plus, the app uses RevenueCat, a purchase service, which receives a random ID created by the app and your App Store purchase history for this app. It doesn't receive your name or email. See RevenueCat's privacy policy.

Notifications

If you allow notifications, the radio alarm schedules a backup chime on your iPhone. These notifications are created on the device; nothing is sent to us.

Children

The app doesn't knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the date above.
