A station doesn't play

Stations sometimes go off the air or change their stream address. Try another station, or tell us which one it is. You can also hide a station from the player's Hide button.

The radio alarm didn't wake me

For the radio to start by itself, leave Airwave Atlas in the background (don't swipe it away) and turn the volume up. If the app was closed, a notification with a chime rings instead; tap it to start the radio. Allow notifications for Airwave Atlas in the iPhone Settings app.

Airwave Atlas Plus

Plus adds the radio alarm, recording, the sleep timer, translated station names and unlimited favorites. To get a purchase back on a new iPhone, open Settings in the app and tap Restore purchases. To cancel the yearly plan, open the iPhone Settings app, tap your name, then Subscriptions.
